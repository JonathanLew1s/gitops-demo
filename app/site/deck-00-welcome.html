How it works

Your turn · about 10 minutes · on your own device

Get on GitHub

  1. Go to github.com/signup and create a free account (or use the one you have).
  2. Verify your email address.
  3. Tell the presenter your first name and your GitHub username.
  4. Watch your inbox for an invitation from GitHub and accept it.
Give people a few minutes. Read the names and usernames aloud; the assistant records them (first names only) and invites them. Names appear on this slide as they are added. CHAT CUE: "Run scene roster-setup", then give the assistant the names and usernames as they come in. PRIVACY: a free account can be deleted afterwards. First names and handles only.
