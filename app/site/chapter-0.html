0. Meet the patient

This page is the demo and the lesson at once. Right now it is being served by the pods you can see above, on a Kubernetes cluster, deployed by a tool that watches a Git repository.

Try it: refresh the page a few times. The pod name in the top right changes, because a Service is spreading your requests across several identical copies.

Three things to notice before we start:

  • The pods above are real, and they are the same things we will later delete, scale and roll.
  • The components table is read from the running software. The scan column is empty on purpose: we have not looked yet.
  • The chapters are locked. Each one unlocks when a change to this page is merged in Git. The lesson literally advances through the workflow it teaches.
